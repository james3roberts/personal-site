============================================================
IMPORTANT PROJECT RULES
============================================================

1. LAUNCH BEFORE PERFECT.

2. SMALL WINS BEAT GIANT PROJECTS.

3. Do not rebuild things that already work without a good reason.

4. The current demo sites are valuable prototype work.
   Do not throw them away.

5. JRC launch comes BEFORE extracting the templates.

6. Templates should eventually make customer work faster,
   not become another giant project.

7. Track actual build time so the process gets better.

8. Don't add complexity just because we can.

9. Build things that customers actually need.

10. Keep JRC human, practical, and a little different
    from generic corporate web agencies.

11. The goal is not simply to have a cool website.
    The goal is to use the website to get customers.

12. Once the site is live, improvement becomes an ongoing
    process rather than a giant "finish the website" project.
